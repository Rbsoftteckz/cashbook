Privacy Policy for CashBook

Last updated: July 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CashBook ("the Application") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect and process information necessary to provide the services offered by the Application. This includes:

  • Google User Data: If you choose to enable Google Drive Backup, the Application accesses your Google Account's appdata folder (specifically, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata) to store and retrieve your ledger backup data safely and securely.
  • Local Data: All ledger transactions, book names, and business profiles are stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Synchronize and back up your ledger database to your personal Google Drive storage.
  • Enable seamless access to your ledger across your authorized devices.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or Google Drive files with any third parties. Your data remains strictly private within your own Google Drive container.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your data. All backup files are stored within your Google Drive's hidden application folder. If you wish to delete your data, you can do so by removing the Application's access through your Google Account permissions, or by deleting the data locally within the app.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at your support email.
