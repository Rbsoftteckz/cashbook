Privacy Policy for Cash Book pro

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Cash Book pro ("the Application") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile bookkeeping and ledger application.

1. Information We Collect & Process

  • Account & Cloud Sync Data: When you sign in or create an account, your email address, business/cashbook records, party balances, team member roles, and transactions are stored in your local device database and securely synchronized with Firebase Cloud Firestore so your authorized devices and invited team members stay in sync.
  • Local Data: Cashbook entries, categories, receipts, and app preferences are stored locally on your device for offline-first access.
  • Optional Permissions:
    • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS): Accessed only when you explicitly choose to import a customer's name and phone number from your phonebook. Contacts are never uploaded in bulk.
    • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely for your scheduled Wasooli / payment collection reminders.
    • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC): Processed strictly on-device by the Android OS if you enable App Lock; biometric data is never accessed or stored by us.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Maintain and calculate your cashbooks, daily ledgers, and party balances.
  • Synchronize and back up your ledger records across your authorized devices and team members.
  • Send locally scheduled payment reminders that you configure.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to third parties. Ledger data is shared only with team members you explicitly invite using your business invite codes.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You control your records and can edit or delete businesses, cashbooks, and entries inside the app at any time. To request deletion of your cloud account data, contact us at Rbmengal@live.com.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at Rbmengal@live.com.
